## Design Context

### Users

Spellbook serves developers and AI power users who maintain skills across Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and similar agents. They use the app during active coding and periodic maintenance to understand what is installed, where it came from, whether copies have drifted, and which action is safe to take next.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, lucid, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a well-made native macOS utility: compact enough for experts, calm enough for extended reading, and precise whenever filesystem changes are involved.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a restrained native macOS visual language with system typography, semantic colors, compact hierarchy, thin dividers, and minimal chrome. Support light and dark appearances equally. Let skill artwork, agent identities, and clear information structure provide personality. Avoid generic AI dashboards, saturated gradients, glowing controls, marketplace framing, excessive cards, fantasy ornament, and sparse layouts that hide useful metadata.

### Design Principles

1. Show local truth first and explain uncertainty rather than implying certainty.
2. Make state actionable with one clear next step and preserve independent installations.
3. Earn every pixel through compact hierarchy, purposeful spacing, and native components.
4. Reuse semantic colors, system type roles, density metrics, and shared interaction patterns instead of one-off styling.
5. Protect user work with previews, recoverable changes, explicit progress, and accessible behavior.
